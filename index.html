AgriGraph

County-scale dairy sustainability · Ireland

A direct platform on the paper Spatio-Temporal Graph Neural Networks for Dairy Farm Sustainability Forecasting and Counterfactual Policy Analysis. ICBF herd records are augmented with a variational autoencoder, scored into four operational pillars, and forecast with a spatio-temporal graph neural network, with a map for benchmarks and counterfactual policy levers.
