## Internship

### Algérie Télécom · DOT Khenchela

I designed and built a billing platform for residential and enterprise subscribers on a local client–server architecture. Node.js, Express, JavaScript, HTML/CSS, and EJS sit on MongoDB collections `comptes`, `compte_corporate`, `corporate_facture`, `conso_client`, and `cycle-facture`, with role-based access for administrative, service, corporate, and client users. The platform handles subscriber management, bimonthly consumption and subscription fees, payment and debt tracking, and dynamic PDF invoices.
